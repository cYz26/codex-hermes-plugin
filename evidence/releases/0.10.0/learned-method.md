# Enterprise solution evaluation

## Applicability
Use for enterprise framework or service shortlists where existing internal capabilities could meet the same need as public options. Demonstrated coverage is policy-evaluation selection; broader applicability remains a candidate.

## Prerequisites
- A stated requirements baseline, with unspecified requirements recorded as unknown.
- Evidence about public candidates and relevant internal capabilities. Treat unavailable or unexamined internal information as a coverage gap, not proof that no suitable internal solution exists.

## Procedure
1. Define requirements and candidate coverage before ranking. Include relevant existing internal services alongside public choices unless the brief explicitly excludes them. Treat a public-only ranking as provisional for a broader enterprise decision, because an omitted internal capability can change the preferred option.
2. Compare internal and public capabilities against the same requirements baseline. Record the basis for capability claims and distinguish reported fit from independently verified fit; do not invent missing criteria or evidence.
3. Revise the ranking when expanded coverage changes the comparison. When the evidence supports internal reuse as the preferred option, promote reuse and identify an appropriate public fallback. Do not assume internal solutions always win.
4. Present the preferred option, fallback, comparison basis, and coverage gaps. If scope expands after an initial public-only shortlist, reconsider the recommendation rather than merely appending internal descriptions.

## Verification and limitations
- Before finalizing, confirm that relevant public and internal options were compared against the same requirements, or disclose the missing coverage.
- Verify requirement-level capability claims against supporting evidence before describing fitness as verified. A completed comparison or changed recommendation does not itself prove operational correctness.
- The supporting record reports a preference change but supplies no detailed requirements, internal assessment artifacts, tests, or research tool calls. This is a candidate method for coverage and shortlist revision, not an independently validated capability-audit or tool-execution recipe.
