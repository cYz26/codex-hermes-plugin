# Enterprise framework and service evaluation

## Applicability and prerequisites

Use for documentation-based enterprise framework or service shortlists where an existing internal capability could satisfy the same requirements. Honor explicit scope restrictions, including public-only evaluations.

Establish the user's requirement baseline and authorized sources for internal capabilities and public candidates. State missing requirements or access gaps rather than inventing coverage.

## Procedure

1. **Set the comparison scope.** Identify required capabilities and whether internal reuse belongs in the candidate set. Keep a public-only ranking provisional when relevant internal options remain unchecked; public documentation cannot establish what the enterprise already has.
2. **Inventory internal and public options before final ranking.** Check existing internal solutions alongside public candidates when both are in scope. If internal discovery is incomplete, explicitly label the shortlist's coverage and record internal capability as unknown, not absent.
3. **Compare against one requirement baseline.** For each decisive capability, record the candidate's coverage and its supporting evidence. Distinguish reported or documented coverage from independently verified coverage; missing evidence is not proof of failure.
4. **Re-rank after adding internal evidence.** Treat an internal capability as eligible for the preferred option when its requirement fit is supported, and retain public options as fallbacks when justified. Do not preserve an earlier public ranking merely because it came first, and do not prefer reuse automatically.
5. **Explain the recommendation and remaining gaps.** State the preferred option, any fallback, and the requirement-based reason for the ranking. If an expanded comparison changes the recommendation, identify the new capability evidence that drove the change. Separate completion of the comparison from verification of the capability claims.

## Verification

- Check that the shortlist covers both internal and public options when both are in scope, uses the same requirements, and explains any ranking change.
- Before calling requirement fit verified, independently check the decisive claims against accessible evidence or project-approved validation. If only result summaries are available, retain reported/unverified wording.

## Limitations

This is a candidate workflow for the stated comparison conditions, not a validated technical selection method. The supporting evidence is summary-level; internal discovery steps, detailed requirements, capability matrices, and independent tests were not supplied. No specific discovery tool, command, or test procedure is established as successful. Inclusion of internal options does not prove technical equivalence, measured superiority, or that reuse always wins.
